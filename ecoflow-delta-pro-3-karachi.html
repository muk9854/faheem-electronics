Yes. At 4,000W rated output with 8,000W on start-up it will start a 1.5 ton split AC, running roughly 1 hr 44 min on a full charge.

How long will the DELTA Pro 3 run a refrigerator?

About 13 hr 56 min for an ordinary fridge, or 23 hr 13 min for an inverter fridge, from its 4,096Wh battery. A fridge cycles on and off, so in practice you will get considerably longer.
