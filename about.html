Our Story

From Electronics to Solar Power

Faheem Electronics started in 2010 as a small electronics shop in Karachi. As energy costs rose, so did our focus — we shifted to what families and businesses needed most: reliable, affordable power.

Today we are a full-service solar company — supplying premium panels, designing systems, managing K-Electric net metering and installing everything from 3kW homes to 100kW+ industrial projects. Over 1,200 systems and 5 megawatts later, our customers' rooftops are our best advertisement.
